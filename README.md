# Aged Care Quality & Regulatory Performance Analytics

## Project overview

This entry-level data analytics portfolio project examines publicly available Australian aged care sector performance data. It focuses on complaints, Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS) incidents and worker regulation outcomes.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics process using Excel, PostgreSQL and Power BI, with an emphasis on data quality, regulatory reporting and evidence-based recommendations.

## Project objectives

* Clean and validate publicly available aged care datasets.
* identify missing values, duplicate records and inconsistent categories.
* Analyse complaint volumes and the most frequently reported concerns.
* Examine trends in SIRS incidents and worker regulation outcomes.
* Calculate relevant quality and performance indicators.
* Document reporting-definition and methodology changes.
* Present findings through a Power BI dashboard and executive summary.

## Key business questions

1. What are the most frequently reported complaint concerns?
2. How have complaint volumes changed across reporting periods?
3. What trends appear in reported SIRS incidents?
4. What worker regulation outcomes are most common?
5. How do residential and home care complaint patterns differ in the legacy data?
6. How do reporting-methodology changes affect comparisons over time?

## Data sources

The project uses aggregated, publicly available sector performance data published by the Australian Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission.

The datasets cover:

* Complaints and complaint concerns
* Serious Incident Response Scheme incidents
* Worker screening and regulation outcomes
* Quarterly and transition reporting periods

No personal or confidential client information is included.

## Tools used

* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, validation, reconciliation and exploratory analysis
* **PostgreSQL:** Data-quality checks, transformations and KPI queries
* **Power BI:** Data modelling, visualisation and dashboard development
* **GitHub:** Project documentation and portfolio presentation

## Data considerations

The aged care reporting model changed following the commencement of the new Aged Care Act on 1 November 2025.

Legacy complaint reports separated residential care and home care, while later reporting consolidated some categories. Complaint classifications and reporting periods also changed.

These differences are documented in the project, and results from different reporting models are not treated as directly comparable without qualification.
